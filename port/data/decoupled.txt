# Game data the PC owns outright, with no N64 address (docs/port/DECOUPLE.md), for tools/port/gen_data.py.
# Format: PC_NAME N64_NAME N64_SIZE [ALIAS ...] [init=image [period=K]]
#
# PC_NAME is the PC's symbol: declared once for the game C (port/include/pc_game_types.h), defined by data.c with
# the host size its declarations give (every reader's must be the same type), zero at startup like BSS, outside the
# arenas and not in the loader's tables. N64_NAME at its N64 address and N64_SIZE bytes from it are retired:
# the arena has no symbol there, and a game object that still references N64_NAME, an ALIAS or any symbol-table
# name in that range fails the build. The range is BSS of the game or the boot segment, or with init=image
# initialized data of one of them: the loader then fills the PC symbol's first elements from those N64_SIZE bytes of
# the image when it loads the image (once, at startup) and leaves the rest zero, or with period=K continues them
# (element i is element i - K). The build holds no value of it.

# The race roster (arcade and MAT3 gLink): struct pc_link[PC_LINK_SLOTS], 13 records of 8 bytes on the N64
# (0x80153E88-0x80153EF0). The game C read [0].owner (+7) with a stride of 8 through a label of its own; the
# image symbol tables also label [0].car, [1].owner and &[6] (likely the end pointer of a 6-slot loop), all
# retired with the range.
pc_links   gLink   0x68

# The model records (MAT3 model[], tModelData): struct pc_model[PC_MODEL_SLOTS], 6 records of 0x808 bytes on the N64
# (0x8014A250-0x8014D280), the same layout on the PC. The alias is the name inside it the game C reached by name,
# model_modelrun (+0x71C, read with a stride of 0x808); it also reached the array's second label and [0]'s
# fields at +0x8, +0x3F0, +0x640, +0x6C4, +0x714, +0x7C4, +0x7C6, +0x7E2 and +0x7EA through labels of the image
# symbol tables, retired with the range, as are the tables' other labels inside it.
pc_models  model  0x3030  model_modelrun

# The car records (MAT3 gGameCar, tCarData, gGameCar): struct pc_car[PC_CAR_SLOTS], 6 records of 0x3B8 bytes on
# the N64 (0x80152818-0x80153E68; sample_dma_pi_queue, a message queue, follows), 0x468 on the PC (docs/port/CARS.md 2). They
# were own storage already (host layout). The aliases are gGameCar and the names inside it the game C reached by
# name: [1]'s car1_objnum (+0), car1_dr_pos (+0x8) and car1_dr_car_uvs (+0x50), read with a stride of 0x3B8 (CAR_OFS
# on the PC). It also reached [0]'s fields at +0xD8, +0xE8, +0xEE, +0xEF, +0xF0, +0x358, +0x35C and +0x3A3 through
# labels of the image symbol tables, retired with the range.
pc_cars    gGameCar  0x1650  gGameCar car1_objnum car1_dr_pos car1_dr_car_uvs

# The effects' per-car arrays (docs/port/DECOUPLE.md 6), 6 cars each on the N64, PC_MAX_LINKS on the PC: the tire-effect
# latches (u32), the smoke's grey (u8; 8 bytes by the gap to gRealJustPlayerData), the trails' last points (f32[6]), the blasts
# (struct pc_blast, 0x190), the blast rings (struct pc_blast_ring, 0x3C; 6 of them to gTempCircuitFirstTrack: the symbol file's
# gCheckpointTimes, gLapCounts, gBestLapTimes and gCurrentLapTimes, inside them, are names nothing uses), the skid
# state of each car's four tires (struct pc_skid: 0x5C on the N64, 0x60 on the PC, its effect pointer a host pointer;
# own storage before) and the resurrect's midpoint (f32[3]).
pc_tire_fx        gCarSmokeDir  0x18
pc_smoke_shade    gCarBlastSmokeClr  0x8
pc_trail_pts      gCarWingSmokePos  0x90
pc_blasts         gBlastObjs  0x960
pc_blast_rings    gBlastRingObjs  0x168
pc_skids          gNewSkid  0x8A0
pc_resurrect_mid  resurrect_save_pos  0x48

# The cars' sounds, checkpoints and finish cameras (docs/port/DECOUPLE.md 7), PC_MAX_LINKS each on the PC, 6 on the
# N64: the engine sound sets (struct pc_engine_sound, 84 B on the N64, own storage before: 88 B here), the screech
# handles (struct pc_sound_rec), each car's last position and checkpoint-plane distance (BSS); and four that the game
# image initializes, filled from it: the sounds-started flags and the finish cameras' state (bytes, 8 by the gap) and
# start times (f32), and the engine update's phase (bytes), whose alternation slots 6 on continue.
pc_engine_sounds    gEngineSounds  0x1F8
pc_screech_sounds   gPaveSkidSounds  0x78
pc_car_sounds_on    gCarActive  6     init=image
pc_engine_phase     gCarUpdatedLastTime  6     init=image period=2
pc_cp_last_pos      gPrevPos  0x48
pc_cp_plane         lastDotProd  0x18
pc_finish_cam_time  finish_cam_stime  0x18  init=image
pc_finish_cam       finish_cam  6     init=image

# The drones (docs/port/DECOUPLE.md 8): MAT3 drones[] and our_drones[] (short[PC_MAX_LINKS]; 6 s16 on the N64,
# 0x801527D8-0x801527E4, the own si_lock_msg after it, and 0x80152808-0x80152814, the car records after the gap), and
# maxpath's controls per car slot (float[PC_MAX_LINKS], float[PC_MAX_LINKS][3]; 6 on the N64): steer, brake, gas
# (0x80153F28, 0x80153F48, 0x80153F68: 24 B each, cur_drone and HAL_DrawText's statics at 0x80153F60 and 0x80153F80
# after them) and avoid_areas' two directions (0x80153F88, 0x80154138: 72 B each, statics at 0x80153FD0, 0x80154180).
pc_drones       drones  0xC
pc_our_drones   our_drones  0xC
pc_mp_steer     lastwheel  0x18
pc_mp_brake     lastbrake  0x18
pc_mp_gas       lastthrottle  0x18
pc_mp_dir       last_dir  0x48
pc_mp_save_dir  last_save_dir  0x48

# CarSetup's flag per car slot (carsetup_flag): signed char[PC_MAX_LINKS], 6 bytes on the N64 (0x801439F8; the gap is

pc_carsetup_flags  carsetup_flag  0x8

# MusyX's per-voice storage (docs/port/DECOUPLE.md 9, AUDIO.md 10): 32 entries on the N64, in the boot segment's BSS,

# N64 layout), the sequence nodes (24 B here, 16 there; own storage before), the priority and free lists' links, an
# effect voice's controllers and its two per-channel bytes (set 255: a voice is its own channel), its legato owner

pc_mx_voice        sound_synth_voices  0x3400
pc_mx_nodes        sound_handle_nodes  0x200
pc_mx_prio_links   sound_voice_priority_links  0x80
pc_mx_free_links   sound_voice_free_links  0x80
pc_mx_fx_ctrl      sound_voice_controllers  0x10C0
pc_mx_fx_chan_a    sound_voice_last_keys  0x20
pc_mx_fx_chan_b    sound_voice_portamento  0x20
pc_mx_fx_legato    sound_voice_channel_owners  0x20
pc_mx_streams      sound_streams  0x300
